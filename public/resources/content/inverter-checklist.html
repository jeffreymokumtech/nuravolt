7 Hidden Causes of Inverter Underperformance

Diagnostic checklist for phase imbalance, MPPT drift & efficiency loss
Purpose: Use this checklist quarterly or when inverter production falls below 90% of expected output. These issues cause 8-15% silent underperformance without SCADA alarms. Check each box after investigating and documenting findings. Keep completed checklists for warranty claims and performance trend analysis.
1 Phase Imbalance

Symptom: One or two phases produce 5-12% less power than the third phase

Diagnostic:

  • Compare 3-phase AC output during midday (11am-1pm) under clear sky conditions
  • Calculate: (Max Phase - Min Phase) / Max Phase × 100%
  • Normal: <3% difference | Warning: 3-8% | Critical: >8%

Action:

  • Check AC contactor condition (contacts may be degraded on one phase)
  • Verify string allocation is balanced across all three phases
  • Inspect phase-specific cable connections and terminations
Impact: 8% phase imbalance = 2.7% total inverter capacity loss (~$2,700/year per MW)
2 MPPT Drift

Symptom: DC voltage consistently 5-15V below optimal operating point

Diagnostic:

  • Record DC voltage during steady-state operation (clear conditions, stable irradiance)
  • Compare to expected Vmp from module datasheet adjusted for temperature: Vmp_actual = Vmp_stc + (Temp - 25°C) × temp_coeff × Vmp_stc
  • Normal: ±5V from expected | Warning: 5-10V | Critical: >10V deviation

Action:

  • Reset MPPT algorithm (power cycle inverter or initiate MPPT re-scan)
  • Check for firmware updates addressing MPPT stability
  • Verify string configuration matches inverter settings (number of modules per string)
Impact: 5% MPPT deviation = 3-5% power loss (~$3,000-$5,000/year per MW)
3 Component Degradation

Symptom: Gradual efficiency decline over months/years without obvious cause

Diagnostic:

  • Calculate monthly DC-to-AC efficiency: (AC Energy Out / DC Energy In) × 100%
  • Plot trend over 12+ months and calculate degradation rate (%/year)
  • Normal: <0.5%/year | Warning: 0.5-1.5%/year | Critical: >1.5%/year

Action:

  • Schedule thermal imaging inspection to identify hot spots (capacitor, IGBT degradation)
  • Review maintenance logs for dust accumulation in cooling system
  • Consider proactive component replacement if degradation accelerating
Impact: 1%/year degradation = $10K cumulative loss over 10-year warranty period per MW
4 Reactive Power Mismanagement

Symptom: Inverter derates active power or fails grid compliance checks

Diagnostic:

  • Compare commanded power factor (from SCADA setpoint) to actual (measured Q/P ratio)
  • Calculate: Actual PF = P / √(P² + Q²) where P=active power, Q=reactive power
  • Normal: <0.02 PF error | Warning: 0.02-0.05 | Critical: >0.05 or grid penalties

Action:

  • Verify reactive power control mode (fixed PF, Q(U), cos φ(P), etc.)
  • Check for conflicting commands from SCADA vs local controller settings
  • Ensure inverter apparent power rating not limiting active power delivery
Impact: 0.05 PF error can reduce active power by 5% + grid compliance penalties ($500-$2,000/incident)
5 Excessive Thermal Derating

Symptom: Power output drops below DC input capacity during high ambient temperatures

Diagnostic:

  • Record ambient temperature, internal inverter temperature, and AC output power
  • Compare to manufacturer derating curve (usually in datasheet or manual)
  • Normal: Matches curve ±5% | Warning: 5-10% more aggressive | Critical: >10% below expected

Action:

  • Check cooling system: fans operational, air filters clean, ventilation unobstructed
  • Review derating settings in inverter configuration (may be set too conservatively)
  • Consider external shading or improved ventilation for inverters in hot locations
Impact: 10% excess derating during peak hours = 3-5% daily energy loss in summer months (~$3,000-$5,000/year per MW)
6 String-Level Mismatch

Symptom: Inverter consistently underperforms vs identical neighbors with same irradiance

Diagnostic:

  • Compare inverter output to 3-5 identical neighbors (same model, string config, exposure)
  • Calculate: (Target Inverter / Avg of Neighbors) × 100%
  • Normal: 95-105% | Warning: 90-95% | Critical: <90%

Action:

  • Inspect DC combiner box connections and fuses for one weak string
  • Check individual string open-circuit voltage (Voc) to identify underperforming string
  • Investigate shading, soiling, or module failures on specific string
Impact: One string 15% underperforming = 5-8% total inverter loss if 8-12 strings (~$5,000-$8,000/year per MW)
7 Intermittent Communication Loss

Symptom: Missing data points during peak production hours, daily totals seem low

Diagnostic:

  • Review SCADA data completeness: count missing timestamps during daylight hours
  • Correlate data gaps with peak irradiance times (10am-2pm most critical)
  • Normal: >99% complete | Warning: 95-99% | Critical: <95% or pattern of peak-hour gaps

Action:

  • Check communication cable quality (RS485/Ethernet) for noise or damage
  • Verify network topology: avoid daisy-chain lengths >1km without repeaters
  • Review SCADA polling frequency and buffer capacity (may be saturated during high data periods)
Impact: 5% data loss during peak hours = 3-4% underestimation of production + false performance issues (~$3,000-$4,000/year per MW)
